========================================
IMPORTANT NOTE
========================================

The GLORYS dataset is a regular gridded ocean
dataset.

Argo observations must NOT be prematurely converted
to the GLORYS grid.

Argo should retain its original:
- measurement locations
- pressure/depth levels
- timestamps
- temperature
- salinity
- float/platform ID
- quality-control flags

Model-observation collocation/interpolation should
be performed later during the comparison stage.
